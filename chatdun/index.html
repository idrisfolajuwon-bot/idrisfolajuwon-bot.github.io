Account & safety
CHATDUN privacy & safety

Information collected: your verified phone number for sign-in; your display name, username and optional profile photo; messages, voice notes, group membership, photo statuses and status-view records; notification settings; and reports you submit.

How it is used: to provide account access, messaging, groups, status sharing, notifications, abuse prevention and support. Your phone number is not displayed to other users. Phone search checks only the number entered and returns a matching CHATDUN profile, not the number.

Storage and visibility: messages and account records are stored by CHATDUN's Supabase backend. Conversation content is intended to be visible only to conversation members. Status visibility follows the selected audience and expires after 24 hours. Voice notes use restricted storage. Profile photos and group photos currently use public image URLs and may be viewable by anyone with the image link, so do not upload private or sensitive pictures there. Status images and voice notes use restricted access rules.

Safety: block accounts and report conversations when needed. Reports are available to the service operator for review. Do not share passwords, verification codes, financial details or private information in chats.

Retention: active account data is retained while the account is in use. Reports may be retained for safety review. Account deletion requests are reviewed and processed separately; submitting a request does not instantly erase the account.

Third-party services: Supabase provides authentication, database, storage and realtime infrastructure. TextBee is used to deliver verification SMS.

Contact: I D LABS support contact details must be published before CHATDUN opens to the public.

Terms & community rules

Use CHATDUN respectfully and lawfully. Do not harass, threaten, impersonate, spam, share others' private information without permission, or upload content you do not have permission to share. Do not use the service to scam or harm people. I D LABS may review reports and restrict accounts that violate these rules. Features may change while CHATDUN is in testing.

Last updated: 13 November 2026. This is an in-app product notice, not legal advice. A full jurisdiction-specific privacy and terms review is still required before public launch.
