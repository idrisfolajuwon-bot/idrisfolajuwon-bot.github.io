What is CHATDUN?

CHATDUN is a web-based messaging experience designed to help people communicate and share. The current build is being developed around one-to-one conversations, group chats, voice notes and status updates. Features and availability may change as development and testing continue.

How does CHATDUN work?

Account setup currently uses phone-number verification. After setting up a profile, users can access the messaging features available in the current test build. Never share your verification code with anyone.

Who develops CHATDUN?

CHATDUN is developed under I D LABS. It is part of the I D LABS product family alongside IDRIS AI STUDIO, an independent AI workspace.
